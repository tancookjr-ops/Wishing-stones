how we like to host

No ticket booth. Just a box by the door.

Pay what you can — the artist keeps every coin in the box. We provide the venue, the warmth and the crowd.

Room for a hundred on folding chairs, if we build out toward David's. Storytellers, movie nights, a full house.

The acoustics? Everyone who plays here swears they're perfect — all that stuff on the shelves softens the sound.
